# Comic Strip Image Prompt — The Bracelet Adventure

Create a cinematic, highly detailed comic strip page (6–8 panels) about an ordinary man whose life changes after accidentally falling into a hidden pit and discovering an ancient bracelet artifact.

## Story Scene
A man accidentally falls into a hidden pit while exploring a remote or abandoned place (dense forest, forgotten ruins, rocky wilderness, or ancient overgrown location). Injured but alive, he discovers an ancient bracelet partially buried in the dirt and glowing faintly.

The moment he picks up the bracelet, he experiences surreal visions of hidden treasures, forgotten civilizations, secret ruins, lost cities, mysterious temples, underground vaults, maps, symbols, and unexplained places from around the world.

Instead of fear, the experience awakens something inside him — his adventurous spirit. He becomes obsessed with uncovering the mysteries shown to him and decides to set off on a journey.

## Comic Panel Breakdown

### Panel 1 — The Fall
Wide cinematic shot of the man accidentally stepping onto unstable ground and falling into a hidden pit. Dirt collapsing, dramatic motion, shocked expression.

### Panel 2 — Trapped Below
The man wakes up injured inside an ancient underground chamber or pit, dim lighting, roots hanging from above, mysterious atmosphere.

### Panel 3 — Discovery of the Bracelet
Close-up of an ancient ornate bracelet partially buried in the soil or hidden between rocks. Strange glow, ancient symbols, mysterious energy.

### Panel 4 — First Contact
The man reaches for the bracelet and touches it. A burst of glowing energy surrounds him.

### Panel 5 — Visions of Secrets
Large dramatic panel showing visions around him:
- Hidden treasure chambers
- Ancient pyramids
- Forgotten ruins
- Lost civilizations
- Secret maps
- Ancient symbols
- Underground temples
- Exotic mysterious locations around the world

The visions should feel magical, mysterious, and incredibly realistic.

### Panel 6 — The Awakening
Close-up of the man looking determined and inspired. The bracelet now worn on his wrist.

### Panel 7 — Beginning the Journey
Wide cinematic ending shot of the man leaving toward the horizon, backpack ready, adventurous expression, mysterious world ahead.

## Visual Style
- Cinematic comic book art
- Hyper detailed
- Adventure-fantasy tone
- Realistic lighting and shadows
- Indiana Jones / Uncharted inspired atmosphere
- Dramatic panel composition
- Rich environmental storytelling
- Consistent protagonist appearance across panels
- Mysterious golden glow from bracelet
- Epic, immersive world-building

## Tone
Mysterious, adventurous, awe-inspiring, cinematic, emotionally engaging.
