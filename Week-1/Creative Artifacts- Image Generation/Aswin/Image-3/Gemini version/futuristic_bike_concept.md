# Futuristic Yellow Sports Bike Concept

## Concept Overview
A conceptual design for a futuristic, yellow sports bike equipped with an advanced rider protection system. The core feature is an automatic side-airbag deployment mechanism designed to act similarly to a car's safety systems (like ABS and standard airbags), specifically protecting the rider and the vehicle during a fall or crash.

## Scene 1: The Bike in Standing Position
* **Vehicle:** A sleek, aerodynamic, highly futuristic sports bike.
* **Color:** Vibrant, bright yellow.
* **Stance:** Parked in a standard standing position, showcasing its aggressive and forward-thinking design lines, futuristic wheels, and advanced display panels.

## Scene 2: Airbag Deployment on Impact
* **Vehicle State:** The bike is shown fallen on its side on the ground.
* **Safety Feature Active:** Large, protective airbags are fully deployed on the exact side the bike has fallen on.
* **Purpose:** This visualizes the safety mechanism in action, demonstrating how the airbags cushion the impact between the bike/rider and the road, minimizing potential damage and injury.
