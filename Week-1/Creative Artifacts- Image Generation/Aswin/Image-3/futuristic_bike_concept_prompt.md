# Futuristic Sports Bike Concept Image Prompt

## Prompt

Create a **highly detailed cinematic concept image** showcasing a **futuristic yellow sports bike** with an innovative **motorcycle airbag protection system**, inspired by how **ABS systems in cars improve rider safety**.

The image should be presented in a **two-scene concept showcase format**:

### Scene 1 — Standing Position
- Show the **futuristic sports bike in a standing position**.
- The bike should appear **sleek, aggressive, aerodynamic, and premium**, resembling an advanced next-generation superbike.
- The bike color should be **vibrant yellow** with **black accents and futuristic detailing**.
- Include **sharp LED lighting**, aerodynamic fairings, futuristic wheel design, and high-tech materials such as carbon fiber and brushed metal textures.
- The motorcycle should look like a **realistic engineering concept prototype**, not cartoonish.
- Environment: **modern futuristic studio or premium industrial concept display setting** with cinematic lighting.
- Camera angle: **3/4 side profile view** to clearly showcase the design.

### Scene 2 — Fall Protection Demonstration
- Show the same bike **fallen on the ground on one side** after a simulated accident.
- On the side where the bike has fallen, **protective airbags are deployed**.
- The airbags should appear to deploy from the side fairings/body of the bike and act as **impact cushions to protect the rider during a fall**.
- The deployed airbags should look **advanced, believable, and integrated into the bike design**, like a next-generation rider safety system.
- The bike should still retain a **premium futuristic sports-bike appearance** even while fallen.
- Add subtle visual storytelling to imply this is a **safety innovation concept**.

### Design Intent
The concept should communicate:
> A futuristic motorcycle safety technology that functions similarly to a car ABS-inspired rider protection system — automatically detecting a fall and deploying side airbags to reduce rider injury.

### Visual Style
- **Ultra realistic**
- **Cinematic product concept render**
- **High-detail industrial design visualization**
- **Futuristic engineering concept**
- **Photorealistic**
- **Premium automotive advertisement style**
- **Dramatic lighting**
- **High contrast reflections**
- **Sharp details**

### Aspect Ratio
Wide cinematic composition suitable for a **concept presentation board or product innovation showcase**.
