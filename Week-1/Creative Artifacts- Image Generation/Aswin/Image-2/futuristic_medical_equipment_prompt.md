# Futuristic Medical Equipment Image Generation Prompt

## Cinematic Detailed Prompt

Create a highly cinematic, ultra-detailed futuristic hospital room scene
set in an advanced medical facility of the near future.

The scene shows a professional doctor standing beside a patient lying on
a medical bed inside a sleek, high-tech hospital room. The doctor is
actively scanning the patient's vital signs using an advanced medical
apparatus attached to the doctor's wrist.

The wrist-mounted apparatus should be the centerpiece of the image and
visually inspired by the reference image: a futuristic, elegant, glowing
wearable device integrated into the wrist and forearm. It should appear
technologically advanced, sleek, ergonomic, and medical-grade rather
than weaponized. The device should project holographic scanning
interfaces and transparent volumetric medical displays.

As the doctor scans the patient, the apparatus emits luminous
holographic waves or layered scanning beams over the patient's body,
visually analyzing vitals in real time. Floating holographic medical UI
elements display: - Heart rate - Oxygen saturation - Blood pressure -
Body temperature - Respiratory rate - Full-body biometric scan

The patient should be lying calmly on a modern hospital bed while subtle
holographic overlays pass over the chest and body, indicating a
non-invasive scan.

-   Premium futuristic materials (glass, titanium, illuminated
