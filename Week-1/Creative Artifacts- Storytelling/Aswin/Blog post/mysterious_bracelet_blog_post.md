# Entry #17 — I Should Probably Stop Writing This (But I Can’t)

I’ve rewritten this post three times.

Every time I get to the part about the bracelet, I sound insane.

So here’s the version that stays closest to the truth.

Three months ago, I was driving through a forgotten stretch of land somewhere no GPS seemed interested in recognizing. Dry earth. Ruined stone walls. The kind of place that feels abandoned long before humans ever arrived. I stopped to stretch my legs, wandered farther than I should have, and the ground disappeared beneath me.

A pit.

Not a dramatic movie fall—just sharp rocks, pain, and the kind of panic that comes when your ankle refuses to cooperate.

At first, I thought I had fallen into old ruins. Broken carvings lined the walls beneath layers of dirt. Something ancient had been buried there for a very long time.

That’s when I found it.

A bracelet.

Dark metal. Too smooth to be old, too strange to feel modern. Symbols etched into the surface shifted when sunlight touched them—or maybe I imagined that part.

I picked it up.

And everything changed.

The first vision hit instantly.

I saw a mountain split open beneath ice, hiding rooms filled with gold older than recorded history. Then a jungle swallowed by fog, where giant stone faces stared toward a river that no map should know about. Underground chambers beneath a city crowded with people completely unaware of what rested below their feet.

I remember collapsing.

I remember vomiting.

I remember hearing whispers in languages I’ve never learned but somehow understood.

Since then, the visions haven’t stopped.

Sometimes they come in dreams.

Sometimes while I’m brushing my teeth.

Sometimes in crowded places where suddenly I’m somewhere else entirely—watching hidden doors open in cliffsides or symbols glow beneath desert sands.

Here’s the terrifying part:

Some places are real.

I tested one.

Just one.

I won’t say where.

But I found something exactly where the bracelet showed me.

No, not treasure.

Something worse.

Proof.

Now I’m stuck between possibilities.

Maybe this thing is supernatural.

Maybe it’s some impossible ancient technology humanity forgot.

Or maybe I hit my head harder than I realized and I’m documenting a very elaborate mental breakdown.

But if I suddenly stop posting—

Check abandoned places.

Look beneath forgotten things.

And if you ever find a bracelet buried where it shouldn’t exist—

Don’t touch it.

Or do.

Part of me thinks that’s exactly what it wants.
